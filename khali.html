Mahatma Gandhi played a signifucant role in India's struggle for independence .He believed that freedom could be achieved thrrough truth,non-violence and peaceful protests.he inspired millions of Indians to unite against Britush rule.

Major Movements

Non-corporatin Movement

• Non-corporation Movement(1920):Gandhi jii encouraged indians to boycott British goods,institutions and services to oppose british rule

He led a historic march to Dandi to protest against the British salt tax through peaceful resistance

Quit India Movement

•Quit India Movement(1942):Gandhi jii launched the movement demanding an end to British rule in India,with the message "Do or Die."
