* About Mahatma Gandhi

Mohandas karamchand Gandhi popularly known as Mahatma Gandhi was one of the greatest leaders of India's freedom struggle.He was born on 2 october 1869 in porbandar Gujarat.He believed that truth,peace and non-violence were the most powerful tools for bringing change in society


Gandhi jii studied law in London and later went to South Africa where the fought against racual discrimination. He introduced the philosophy of Satyagraha which became a powerful method of peaceful resisitance


After returning to India,he became a leading figure in the freedom movement and inspired millions of people to stand against British rule through non-violent protests .His principles of truth,simplicity,equality and self-reliance continue to inspire people around the world


His life was not only a struggle for freedom but also a message of humanity,peace and justice.
