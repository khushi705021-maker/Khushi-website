Biography of Mahatma Gandhi

Early life

Mahatma Gandhi was born on 2ndoctober 1869 in porbandar,Gujarat.His father was Karamchand Gandhi and mother was Putlibai Gandhi

Education

Gandhi ji studied law in london and became a barreister in 1891

Family

Gandhi ji married kadturba Gandhi in 1883.They had four sons

Freedom Struggle

Gandhi ji played a significant role in India's freedom movement.He led important movements such as the Non-corporation Movement,the Dandi March and tbe Quit IndiavMovement.His peaceful resistance inspired millions of indians

His thoughts and Beliefs

Gandhi ji believed in truth,non-violence simplicity and self-reliance.He introduced the idea of Satyagraha,which emphasized resisting injustice through peaceful means.His teachings encouraged people to live with compassion and dignity

His Legacy

Mahatma Gandhi's ideas continue to inspire people across the world.His commitment to peace,equality and non-violence influence many leaders and movements.His life remains a symbol of courage,simplicity and humanity.
